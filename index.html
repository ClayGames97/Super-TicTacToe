Super Tic-Tac-Toe

Win three small boards in a row to take the big board. Your move sends your opponent to the matching small board next.

A small board stays open until all nine boxes are filled — that's just a visual cue, not a requirement. The instant you complete a three-in-a-row in a board, it counts toward your big-board win, even if your opponent later lines up in that same board too.
